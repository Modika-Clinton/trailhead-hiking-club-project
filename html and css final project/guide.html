Falcon Ridge Trail Guide

A 12 km circular route with GPS-friendly waypoints. Total evolution gain is 500 m 640 m after the 2026 resurvey. Allow 5½ hours, and carry 2 L of H2O.

Warning: the ridge section is not suitable in high wind.

"Start early: the ridge is after noon"

Route stages

  1. Car park tobirch Wood
  2. Birch Wood to the saddle
  3. Saddle to summit cairn → lunch
    4. Descent via the old quarry
