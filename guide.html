Falcon Ridge Trail Guide

A 12 km circular route with GPS-friendly waypoints. Total evolution gain is 500 m
640 m after the 2026 resurvey. Allow 5½ hours, and carry 2 L of H2O.

Warning: the ridge section is not suitable in high wind.

Start early: the ridge is exposed after
noon.

Route stages

  1. Car park to Birch Wood
  2. Birch Wood to the saddle
  3. Saddle to summit cairn → lunch
  4. Descent via the old quarry

Gear

  • All year
    • Boots
    • Rain jackets
  • Winter
    • Microspikes
    • Head torch

PACK CHECKLIST

[x] water [x] map [ ] spare socks

Glossary

Cairn
a stack of stones making the route.

Saddle
Low point between two packs.

Scree
Loose stories on a slope.

Waypoint
A named point on the route.
